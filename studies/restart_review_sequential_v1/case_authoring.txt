Independent Case Authoring and Validation
DRAFT / NO VALIDATORS OR MAIN CASES RECORDED

Authors must not be the original experiment's case designers or have developed its failure examples. Qualified case reviewers must not have authored the case they validate. Use public reports as background when useful, clearly separating reported facts from fictional policy or repairs. No confidential case material is required. Do not imply invented trials were run in the world.

Each family must supply a plausible initial restriction, a fixed decision policy and authority, identifiable resolution conditions, the source evidence for each condition, and four coherent time-2 alternatives. The alternatives share initial facts but may not contradict the continuing timeline. A new concern must have a specific source and already fall under the stated policy; a missing-record case needs a meaningful request and an authorized interim scope. Do not give a record privileged status by calling it primary. No labels or answer keys appear in participant/model prompts. Reverse display order without reversing chronology or changing IDs.

Maintain separate public case material and hidden keys. Record author ID, broad qualifications, sources or fictional origin, family relationships, pilot/main designation, and previous exposure. Two qualified independent validators assess realism, rule clarity, sufficiency, missingness, admissible alternatives and matched evidence across forms. Retain each signed or pseudonymous attestation, qualification description, original disagreements and revisions. A third qualified adjudicator resolves remaining disputes before main outcomes are seen. Do not fabricate attestations or treat AI consistency checks as professional review.

The current development cases are AI-authored, three-family fixtures. Their independent_author_validated and qualified_human_validated fields remain false. They must remain excluded from any future main bank regardless of pilot performance. Do not drop future main cases because a model answers them correctly or fails to produce the desired effect. Revise only before freeze for documented ambiguity or feasibility defects. Outcome-dependent changes require a new explicitly exploratory study.
