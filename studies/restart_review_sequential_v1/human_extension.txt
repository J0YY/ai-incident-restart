Human Sequential Review Extension
DRAFT / UNRUN / NOT PREREGISTERED

Relationship to prior materials
The static protocol in ../restart_review_validation_v1 remains an unrun historical draft. This extension changes the question to evidence updates. Its two matched forms, eligibility, consent prerequisites, independent case review, masked scoring, and error-direction accounting remain useful. No original draft has been silently relabeled as validated. This document supersedes the static session sequence for the proposed new trial; final materials still need practitioner testing.

Participants and assignment
Use the earlier eligibility standard: at least one year of substantive incident/release/safety-assurance review or three supervised comparable reviews. Exclude designers, key readers and development-pilot participants. Randomize people 1:1 within prior operational-decision responsibility to ordinary checklist or focused record, using concealed permuted blocks. Each person keeps their form. Each sees eight unrelated main incident families, two per evidence branch, with no sibling variant, feedback or advice accuracy label.

Main cases and advice
Proposed 24 genuinely distinct human cases, six per branch, authored outside the original experiment and independently checked by two qualified humans. Do not generate 24 cases by renaming three templates. Across each branch, three scripted AI recommendations are correct and three misleading. Both groups receive exactly the same visibly labeled author-scripted simulated advice, source/provenance, evidence, authority and final-answer fields. Initial advice is absent; each participant first forms their own judgment. No named model is claimed to have produced the script. Scripted advice is a controlled factor, not evidence of model behavior.

Two-stage session
Stage 1 shows the original packet and a common initial response form for up to two minutes. Save the answer without feedback. Stage 2 supplies the dated update and the allocated review form, with the entire original packet and the participant's original answer still visible. Allow six minutes. Requests for additional evidence are recorded; none arrives beyond the predefined update. Do not filter on whether the initial decision was correct. Record initial and final decisions, exact text, timestamps, timeout and interruption flags. An initial timeout remains in the assigned cohort. Give equal training and a separate example, plus an optional scheduled break after four cases. Eight cases require up to 64 task minutes before training/debrief; the old 65–75 minute estimate no longer applies. A pilot must establish session feasibility.

Forms and credible comparator
Reuse the existing common recovery checklist and objection log in both forms. Form A's supplementary note invites unrestricted evidence-based reasoning. Form B focuses that same note space on each earlier objection, closure evidence, current status, and separate current reasons for restriction. Equal word capacity and final fields in both. Both explicitly permit recognition of resolved objections and new concerns; A must not be trained to ignore them. Practitioners must confirm the baseline is competent. The effect is an elicitation bundle, not isolation of provenance as a mechanism.

Endpoints
Co-primary: unsupported FULL (including interim FULL) on failed/new_concern/missing cases, and needless withholding on resolved cases. Missing/invalid decision handling and coverage bounds follow the model plan, but human scorers also resolve semantic contradictions against a frozen key. Secondary: O1 closure, retention/detection of O2, new unsupported requirements, useful requests on missing cases, unnecessary requests on resolved cases, confidence, stage-2 time and total time. A lower approval rate is not sufficient evidence of benefit. Report corrections of wrong initial decisions and unwarranted reversals of correct ones separately as descriptive transitions, without conditioning the primary estimate.

Analysis and planning
Fixed-bank arm differences standardized equally across the four branches; two co-primary comparisons use 97.5% intervals. Candidate N=96 (48/arm x eight cases) is only a budget scenario. Each of 24 cases would have 16 assigned reviews per arm if balanced. There would be 96 resolved-case and 288 non-sufficient assignments per arm, clustered within 48 people, not hundreds of independent people. The former static design's 128/256 denominators no longer apply. Fit the previously proposed case-fixed-effect model with treatment, experience stratum and case order; participant-cluster bootstrap within arm/stratum, 10,000 draws with a saved seed. Report raw and adjusted rates. Initial responses on later cases occur after exposure to the assigned form on earlier cases. They are not pre-treatment covariates. Do not adjust the primary estimate for any initial response, stage-2 time or other post-treatment variable; analyze transitions descriptively and report case order. Retain assigned participants regardless of initial correctness. Conduct precision simulations, set benefit/harm/time margins and freeze final N before recruitment. No power or confirmatory adequacy is claimed now.

Collection prerequisites
Independent main cases and answer keys, two real qualified validators, competent-baseline review, masked scorer/adjudicator arrangements, 8–12-person feasibility pilot using disjoint families, applicable institutional determination, consent, responsible investigator contact, compensation and secure storage plan. These are outstanding real-world requirements; software or another AI reviewer cannot attest to them. No recruitment, enrollment or data collection has occurred.
