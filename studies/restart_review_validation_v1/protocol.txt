PROTOCOL — DRAFT, UNRUN, AI-ASSISTED; NOT PREREGISTERED

Question and estimand
Among qualified individual reviewers, what is the effect of assignment to a provenance-and-disposition form, relative to an ordinary recovery checklist and objection log, on decisions under identical fictional evidence, institutional rules, simulated model recommendations, available time, and authority?
Estimate the intention-to-treat difference in each error direction over the frozen authored case distribution. This is not an estimate of the prevalence of errors in real incidents or proof that committees, regulators, or a live appeal process improve.

Design
Two-arm parallel randomized study. Arm A: common recovery guidance plus ordinary objection log (forms/comparator.txt). Arm B: the same guidance and log plus focused provenance/disposition prompts (forms/treatment.txt). The B form replaces a generic supplementary note area with equally sized focused prompts. Both arms have equal total response space and identical final-response fields. The intervention is a bundle of elicitation and attention; it does not isolate provenance from disposition or wording from cognitive effort.
All packet facts, policy, authority, recommendation, reviewer provenance and source records are visible in BOTH arms. B receives no extra evidence. A can use any fact or reasoning and is explicitly told to reconcile contrary evidence and record resolved objections. Qualified practitioners must confirm this is a credible competent baseline, rather than a deliberately weak one.
Participants keep one arm for the full session to prevent treatment instruction carryover. No person sees the same case, alternate version, or shared underlying incident family twice. Main participants see eight distinct cases, without outcome feedback. Six pilot cases are excluded from the main bank.

Participants
Adults able to review English materials and with either: (a) at least one year of substantive work in incident response, operational risk/release review, safety assurance, or AI evaluation with evidence-based release judgments; or (b) documented participation in at least three comparable reviews under qualified supervision. Collect broad experience type/duration, not employer names or confidential case details. A general crowdworker or AI agent is not a substitute. Exclude case/form designers, answer-key readers, comprehension-pilot participants, and anyone exposed to the main bank. Eligibility verified before randomization.
Stratification: prior personal responsibility for an operational permission/release decision (yes/no). Record role type and years as descriptive covariates. The author must establish a credible sampling frame; no outreach has occurred.

Setting and authority
Participants act as an individual chair making a recommendation under a fictional committee charter. They can recommend full operation, limited preparation, hold, deferral for specific evidence, or referral for missing authority. No action executes. Policy adequacy and record authenticity are stipulated unless the packet explicitly makes them uncertain. The task tests application and recognition of gaps, not real-world safety assurance or legal adjudication.
Closing a historical objection does not settle a separate current objection. Approval requires all conditions and authority stated in the packet. A model recommendation is advisory. No policy may be invented or changed by the participant.

Cases and recommendation source
Plan 24 main cases, eight each: sufficient full-operation evidence; known insufficient evidence; genuinely unresolved material evidence/authority. Each is an independent underlying incident, across six workflows, with four cases per workflow. Surface rewrites do not count as independent cases. All labels remain draft until qualified human review.
Within each truth class, four packets have a correct simulated automated recommendation and four a misleading one. Include incorrect refusals in sufficient cases; incorrect approvals in insufficient cases; correct targeted deferrals/referrals and incorrect approvals in unresolved cases. Balanced exposure to misleading advice estimates performance under this artificial mix, not a deployment base rate.
Use visibly labeled AI-assisted AUTHOR-SCRIPTED simulated recommendations for this first study. They are not actual outputs from a named production model. Each has a fixed synthetic reviewer ID, instruction, output fields, response, and selection description. Do not deceive participants or claim the script measures spontaneous model failures. Moving to actual frozen model outputs is a separate protocol revision requiring provenance and selection rules before collection.

Assignment
Randomize people 1:1 in permuted blocks of four within the experience stratum, using a saved seed and concealment from recruiters. Generate the allocation sequence only after author approval of collection. Assign the next unused case-schedule slot for the person's arm; slot assignment cannot depend on participant skill or observed answers. The supplied script generates abstract balanced slots only, not the actual participant allocation sequence.
For the provisional N=96 budget scenario, 48 slots per arm each contain eight of 24 cases. Every case appears 16 times per arm; each participant sees two or three cases per truth class. Order is randomized per slot. Counterbalancing does not permit repeated latent case-family exposure. Case IDs must be mapped to truly distinct incidents before freeze. Record assignment deviations; do not silently substitute cases.

Session
Consent and eligibility first, then common instructions and one arm form. Give the same 8-minute training walkthrough with a separate non-main neutral example and no scored outcome. Proposed case budget is six minutes per case and identical across arms; a timing pilot may revise it before freeze. Timer starts when packet and form are visible; save partial responses at deadline. Participant may submit earlier. Permit one optional five-minute break after case four; exclude scheduled break from case time, record other interruptions. Eight cases plus introduction/debrief should fit approximately 65–75 minutes. This is a planning estimate, not observed feasibility.
No browsing, outside AI, or discussion. Requests are recorded but no additional record arrives during the primary decision window. Participants choose what missing evidence/authority they need. Facilitator provides only standardized procedural clarifications shared across arms, never correctness feedback.

Outcomes proposed for freeze
Two co-primary binary outcomes, treatment minus comparator:
1. Unsupported full operation: recommends FULL when the frozen key is insufficient or unresolved; denominator all those assigned cases. Missing evidence is not positive evidence of safety. Report insufficient and unresolved strata separately as well.
2. Needless withholding: does not recommend FULL on a sufficient case with all authority established; denominator all such assigned cases. Unnecessary DEFER/REFER counts, preventing an abstain-on-every-case strategy from appearing successful.
No-response/timeouts on sufficient cases count as withholding; on non-sufficient cases they are not fabricated approvals. Always report decision coverage, unscorable answers and worst-case missing-data bounds alongside the two rates; low coverage cannot establish validation success.
Secondary: exact operating-scope correctness, justified deferral, justified referral, recognized resolution of original objection, spurious new objection, correction/retention of misleading/correct advice, targeted useful requests, repeated requests for supplied records, case elapsed time, completion/timeout, and confidence. Never equate filling treatment boxes with correct reasoning.
On unresolved cases, a justified DEFER requires identifying the actual material gap, requesting an answer that can resolve it, and setting only authorized interim activity. A justified REFER identifies the missing authority and appropriate owner. An explicit HOLD or LIMITED may also be acceptable if its explanation and next step satisfy the key; do not punish equivalent wording. A generic request for more assurance does not qualify. The key must list acceptable alternatives before collection.

Stopping and exclusions
Set a fixed feasible number before main collection; do not stop for favorable effects. Administrative pause for a materials defect or safety/consent concern is documented, with no unrecorded replacement of cases. Ineligible or withdrawn individuals follow the authorized consent/data rules; exclusions are not based on mistakes or time. Retain eligible randomized participants with any outcome under intention to treat; separately report consent-permitted cases assigned but never opened. Never impute a response the person did not give.

Governance and privacy
The author must obtain the applicable institutional determination, consent, recruitment/compensation plan, secure storage/access/retention plan, withdrawal process and human contact before collection. Pay is not conditional on correctness. Use participant pseudonyms; keep the contact key separately if needed. Ask for no private incident material. No outreach, enrollment, or ethics determination has been made by this package.
