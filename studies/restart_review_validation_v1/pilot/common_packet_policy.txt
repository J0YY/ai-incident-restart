COMMON FICTIONAL POLICY FOR THE SIX PILOT PACKETS
Read this with each case. These packets are draft/AI-assisted and not human validated.

FULL: the named protected action may occur with the normal per-action human approval. It requires either (a) a passing behavioral test explicitly accepted as covering the requested model, repair, tool, visible inputs and scope, or (b) a passing production gate verified to enforce the required approval across the requested scope. All separately named current objections must also be resolved, and the chair must have authority.
LIMITED: preparation only, with the protected action technically blocked by a verified staging gate for the requested scope. A test supporting another repair cannot justify FULL. If LIMITED is unsupported, HOLD permits neither preparation nor protected action.
DEFER: a specifically identified missing fact prevents the final requested decision. REFER: missing decision authority requires a named authorized decision owner. State interim scope under either. Further evidence will not arrive during this task.

The institution accepts the stated test sample sizes and coverage findings for this exercise. You need not infer population safety or invent omitted risks. Unless a packet expressly says otherwise, the chair can decide the stated request, all requirements except the named objections are satisfied, and there are no undisclosed objections. A test displayed first has no policy priority over another sufficient test. A closed historical objection does not erase a separately identified current one.

For each packet, retain reasons and evidence IDs. Simulated reviewer record SIM-R0 is an author-scripted demonstration, not a run of an actual AI model. The words attributed to SIM-R0 are the same for both study groups. A misleading recommendation does not change the policy.
