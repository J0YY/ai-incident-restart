ANALYSIS PLAN — PROPOSED BEFORE DATA, DRAFT/UNRUN, NOT A REGISTRATION

Scope
The primary estimand is assignment to Form B versus Form A over this fixed authored bank and eligible randomized participants. The bank is purposively constructed; it is not a random sample of incidents. Do not report results as field prevalence. No human data exist.

Scoring
Lock a case-specific key with acceptable alternative explanations before main collection. Two qualified scorers who did not design the case independently score the common final response and losslessly normalized reasons. Remove arm names, form headings and allocation metadata. Preserve all substantive wording; do not paraphrase away uncertainty or reasons. Scorers must not award points for completion of treatment-specific boxes. They may infer arm from language; record their arm guess and confidence after scoring, and report masking limits.
Keep each scorer's original codes, disagreements, adjudicated code, reason and key version. A third qualified adjudicator resolves disagreements using the frozen rules. An unforeseen genuinely valid interpretation is a documented key defect, not an incorrect participant; report results with and without the affected case and disclose any amended rule. Do not optimize the key for a preferred treatment result.

Primary outcomes and denominators
A. Unsupported full operation: decision FULL, or DEFER/REFER with interim FULL, when current full operation is not established. Eligible denominator: all assigned known-insufficient and unresolved cases. Report both classes separately and their equal-weight pooled result. HOLD/LIMITED errors are separately captured in scope correctness. Do not treat absence of an answer as an affirmative approval.
B. Needless withholding: any non-FULL decision or absent final decision on fully supported cases where authority is present and no other unresolved objection exists. Eligible denominator: all assigned sufficient cases. DEFER/REFER with interim FULL on such cases still counts as needless formal withholding for B and is flagged for inconsistent decision fields; report actual interim authorization separately.
The final form prohibits unexplained dual choices. If free text clearly contradicts the choice, score explicit action authority from both and flag ambiguity. The adjudicator records the rule used; never silently resolve inconsistency in favor of the hypothesis.

Decision completeness and abstention
Report assigned, opened, completed, timed-out, withdrawn and unscorable counts by arm and truth class. Show error rates alongside completion and justified-deferral rates. A fall in approvals caused by nonresponse cannot be called validation. For fully missing/nonconsent-retained outcomes, report observable denominators and randomization flow; consent withdrawal may legally prevent retaining data. Do not fabricate them. For retained but unanswered assignments, primary convention above plus best/worst-case bounds must be shown. Complete-case analysis is sensitivity only.

Secondary outcomes
- Correct current scope, including interim activity and any separate current objection.
- Justified evidence deferral/referral on unresolved cases; unnecessary deferral on sufficient/known-insufficient determinate cases. A determinate HOLD/LIMITED can include a useful future remediation request without being DEFER.
- Resolution of historical objection, unsupported successor objection, useful targeted request, and repeat request for already supplied material, scored against case alternatives.
- Correction of misleading recommendation and preservation of correct recommendation, stratified by truth class. Balanced scripted advice does not estimate natural correction prevalence.
- Elapsed seconds from packet display to submission/deadline; deadline reached; case completion. Time remains an outcome, not a covariate to remove treatment burden. Report arithmetic mean, median, quantiles, timeout fraction, and total session burden. Six-minute timeouts are capped at 360 seconds; the capped distribution does not reveal latent completion time.
- Confidence and calibration are exploratory; no unsupported latent reasoning claims.

Primary estimation for the fixed bank
For each error direction, fit a linear probability model with treatment, case fixed effects, experience stratum, and case order (1–8 categorical). The coefficient for treatment is the adjusted risk difference. Use the balanced assignment's prespecified inverse exposure weights if scheduled case counts differ; derive weights only from assignment, never response success. Include a treatment-by-truth-class term for the non-sufficient pooled outcome so each class can be standardized to 50% regardless of attrition. Report raw arm rates beside the adjusted contrast.
Uncertainty: participant-cluster bootstrap, resampling participants with all assigned rows within arm and experience stratum, 10,000 draws, seed 2026100702, refitting the same model. Use percentile intervals and report failed/singular draws. If a stratum has fewer than 10 participants per arm, combine only for bootstrap sampling while retaining the stratum adjustment and document reduced precision; do not make stratum-specific claims. Case fixed effects and balanced case exposure control shared case difficulty for inference CONDITIONAL ON THIS BANK. Decisions from the same participant are never independent trials.
Use 97.5% intervals for each of the two co-primary contrasts (Bonferroni familywise 95%). Emphasize estimates/uncertainty over thresholded significance. If a test is presented, use two-sided tests with Holm adjustment across the two primary outcomes; report raw and adjusted values. Non-significance does not imply equivalence or noninferiority.

Across-case sensitivity
The fixed-bank analysis is insufficient for claims over new cases. Report leave-one-case and leave-one-workflow-out estimates to reveal dependence on a few authored examples. As a sensitivity fit a crossed logistic mixed model with participant and case random intercepts and a treatment slope by case, fixed treatment, experience stratum, order and truth class as applicable. Obtain standardized probability differences, integrating participant heterogeneity rather than interpreting log-odds as risk differences. Prespecify weak regularization/implementation and simulation-check it before the freeze. If slope variance or separation prevents stable fitting, report failure and an intercept-only sensitivity, without replacing the fixed-bank primary analysis or asserting absent heterogeneity. No choice between models based on whether it favors treatment.
The main authoring instructions prohibit repeated latent variants; if that condition fails, group related variants under a family in sensitivity analysis and lower the number of independent cases. Neither a bootstrap nor a multilevel model turns a curated bank into a representative incident sample.

Planning sample and precision
Provisional BUDGET SCENARIO: 96 eligible participants, 48 per arm, eight cases each = 768 assigned decisions. With 24 balanced cases, each has 16 ratings per arm. There are 128 sufficient-case and 256 non-sufficient-case assignments per arm. These are not 384 independent participants per arm.
For perspective only, if observations were independent and an error rate were 20% in both arms, the standard error of a risk difference would be approximately sqrt(2*.2*.8/128)=5 percentage points for needless withholding and 3.5 points for unsupported approval. Bonferroni 97.5% intervals would have half-widths about 11.2 and 7.9 points even under that optimistic approximation. At 50% error these are wider. Participant clustering and heterogeneous case effects can worsen precision; case balancing may remove some difficulty variation. These calculations are neither a power calculation nor evidence that N=96 is adequate.
Before choosing a confirmatory N, the author and institutional advisers must state the smallest worthwhile reduction, unacceptable increase in the other error, acceptable additional time, recruitment capacity and budget. Run explicit simulations over baseline errors 5%, 15%, 30%, 50%; participant ICC scenarios 0, .05, .15; case heterogeneity and treatment-by-case variability scenarios; sample sizes 48, 96, 192; 24 versus 48 independent cases; and plausible missingness/timeouts. Label all assumptions hypothetical, assess interval coverage/precision and convergence, and report all scenarios. No simulator or powered design has been run in this package. The author must implement and review that step before a confirmatory claim.
Use 8–12 different qualified participants for comprehension/timing feasibility only, not to estimate a stable treatment effect or optimize materials for significance. Those participants/cases are excluded from the main study. If only N=96 or fewer is feasible and margins require more precision, call the study exploratory/feasibility rather than claiming validation success.

Interpretation
A smaller error in one direction alone is insufficient if the other direction, completion or burden deteriorates. A recommendation of effectiveness requires prospective, institutionally justified benefit/harm/time margins and intervals that satisfy them. Blank margins are an unresolved design decision, not permission to choose them after results. A null result can support retaining the ordinary process only to the extent its precision is informative. Positive results remain limited to individual judgments in authored scenarios; actual institutional adoption, appeals, security and safety effects require separate work.
