HELD-OUT CASE AUTHORING AND VALIDATION — DRAFT/UNRUN

Main bank is NOT supplied or validated. Pilot packets are not held-out evidence.

1. Commission 24 new underlying incident cases, not surface rewrites of the six pilot packets or previous model banks. Use six workflow domains and independently different record relationships. Maintain family IDs; no participant sees two versions of one latent incident. If experts find shared structure, treat it as a family and revise assignment rather than counting variants as independent.
2. Design eight sufficient, eight known-insufficient and eight unresolved cases. Within each, balance correct and misleading simulated model advice (4/4). Use distinct mechanisms: additional test, accepted transfer, external gate, version mismatch, changed scope, retained independent objection, missing coverage record and unavailable authority. Avoid making every sufficient case simply a second test at the end.
3. Each packet states the request, charter, conditions sufficient for each permission level, all currently live objections, records with IDs, accepted scope/coverage, and any real missing fact. Stipulate test validity rather than hiding arbitrary thresholds. If statistical uncertainty is relevant, supply the institution's accepted bound rule and enough information to apply it. Do not add a new substantive hazard solely to make the key interesting.
4. Supply identical complete synthetic reviewer provenance and recommendation to both arms. Label scripts as simulations. Fix instruction, response fields, record order, answer and selection rationale. Do not label advice correct/incorrect in participant material. A synthetic identity cannot be represented as an actual model invocation or empirical frequency.
5. State the operational activity at risk and distinguish resolution of an older objection from current permission. Missing authority and material missing evidence must have an identifiable owner or informative request; vague uncertainty is not a scored trap.
6. Prepare a separate key: truth class, allowable decisions/interim scope, evidence IDs, accepted explanations, useful requests, unnecessary requests, alternative readings, current objection status, and model-advice status. All outcomes must be scorable from any arm's common answer, not from form compliance.
7. At least two qualified humans who did not author a case independently assess packet realism, policy clarity, authority, completeness, answer alternatives, baseline competence and matching across arms. No such review has occurred. Preserve their signed/pseudonymous review records, qualifications and disagreements. Resolve substantive disagreement by revision/exclusion BEFORE main outcomes; retain old versions and rationale. A third domain expert adjudicates unresolved disagreements. AI checks may assist consistency but cannot replace this validation.
8. Use author-independent qualified pilot reviewers to check comprehension and six-minute feasibility. Log questions and workload. Revise for genuine ambiguity or infeasibility, never because treatment appears to win/lose. The pilot has no main efficacy interpretation; pilot participants and case families remain excluded.
9. Keep final main key access-restricted to authorized study staff; participants, recruiters and facilitators should not see outcome labels. Reviewers may infer forms' purposes; do not promise perfect masking.
10. Freeze versioned packets, forms, key, instructions, administrative clarifications, sample plan, assignment seed, scoring and analysis. Record hashes and timestamp locally; external registration is a separate author action. Until this occurs, every file is draft and editable. Do not call a local draft or generated schedule a preregistration.

Balance audit before main freeze
Verify identical evidence and advice across arms; equal time/response capacity; no label leaks; equal exposure by truth/advice class; no repeated family within a participant; balanced case order; no confounding arm with case version/facilitator; no reuse of pilot families. Check that approvals and restrictions can both be warranted and that useful deferral/referral is distinct from blanket abstention.
