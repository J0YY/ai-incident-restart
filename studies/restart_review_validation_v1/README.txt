Restart review validation v1
Status: DRAFT / UNRUN / AI-ASSISTED. Created 7 October 2026.

No participants have been recruited, contacted, or studied. No human case validation, institutional approval, preregistration, or results are claimed. This package is preparation for author and qualified-expert review.

Purpose
Test whether adding model-review provenance and an explicit objection disposition to an ordinary recovery checklist and objection log improves qualified reviewers' decisions. This is an individual decision exercise, not a test of actual committee authority, appeals, or operational safety.

Read protocol.txt, analysis_plan.txt, and case_authoring_and_validation.txt first. Participant materials are forms/common_instructions.txt, one assigned form, and one pilot/case_*.txt packet at a time. pilot/answer_key.json is FACILITATOR ONLY and has not been independently human validated. facilitator_guide.txt explains administration. Data templates contain headers only, no observations. scripts/make_schedule.py creates a proposed main-study slot schedule with abstract case identifiers; it is not a participant roster or an allocation of real people.

Six pilot cases are complete fictional practice materials. They are excluded from any main outcome study, as are people exposed to them in the comprehension pilot. A new held-out, expert-checked main bank is still required. No outcome collection should begin until the author completes consent, research-review determination, compensation, data handling, eligibility screening, and qualified review of cases and the baseline.

Relationship to earlier drafts
Read during preparation: results/incident_practitioner_study/protocol.txt, README.txt, common instructions, arms B/C and facilitator guide; results/public_incident_evidence_audit/recipient_study_protocol.txt. Those drafts use three arms and, in one design, sequential changes and rescue probes. This package retains their strong baseline, masked scoring, separate error directions, and no repeated case-family exposure. It uses TWO arms, one final decision per case, and identical simulated automated-review material in both arms. It does not implement staged policy changes, rescue probes, enforced deadlines, reassignment, or appeals. These designs must not be pooled. Earlier files are unmodified.

Remaining author work
1. Select the institution and verify research arrangements; name a human principal investigator and contact.
2. Have qualified reviewers assess forms and pilot cases; resolve disagreements without optimizing arm differences.
3. Conduct a comprehension/timing pilot only after required arrangements; no recruitment is authorized by this package.
4. Author and independently validate held-out main cases; freeze case versions and scoring alternatives.
5. Set worthwhile benefit, harm, time margins and a feasible sample; run sensitivity simulations before claiming adequate precision.
6. Freeze and, if desired, publicly register the final plan before main collection. This draft is not such a registration.
